Safety Tips for Natural Disasters

Stay safe during natural disasters like floods, earthquakes, and hurricanes.

What to Do During Different Natural Disasters

What to do during a flood or flash flood

  • Move immediately to higher ground or stay on high ground.
  • Continue to check the media for emergency information.
  • Follow instructions from public safety officials. If advised to evacuate, do so immediately.
  • If you must evacuate your home, take only essential items and bring your pets if safe to do so.
  • Do not walk through flowing water. Six inches of swiftly moving water can knock you off your feet.
  • Remember: "Turn Around, Don’t Drown!" Don’t drive through flooded roads. Cars can be swept away in only two feet of moving water.

What to do during an earthquake

  • If you are in a car, pull over and stop. Set your parking brake.
  • If you are in bed, turn face down and cover your head and neck with a pillow.
  • If you are outdoors, stay outdoors away from buildings.
  • If you are inside, stay inside and avoid doorways.
  • Expect aftershocks to follow the main shock of an earthquake. Be ready to Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • If you are in a damaged building, go outside and quickly move away from the building. Do not re-enter.

What to do during a hurricane

  • Avoid driving or going outdoors during a storm. Flooding and winds can make traveling dangerous.
  • Do not walk through flowing water. Six inches of swiftly moving water can knock you off your feet.
  • Remember the phrase "Turn Around, Don’t Drown!" Don’t drive through flooded roads.
  • Stay out of damaged buildings until authorities deem them safe.
  • If you believe there is a gas leak, go outdoors immediately, and do not turn electrical switches or appliances on or off.

Stay Alert! Stay Safe!
